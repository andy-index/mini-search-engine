A/B Testing and Experimentation Frameworks

A/B testing, or split testing, is a quantitative user research methodology used to compare two versions of a webpage, feature, or app interface against a baseline to determine which performs better. In a standard A/B test, users are randomly divided into control and variant groups. Statistical hypotheses test whether variations produce statistically significant differences in target conversion metrics, such as click-through rates (CTR) or purchase completions. Modern product teams leverage experimentation platforms to manage feature flags, control rollout percentages, and run multivariate tests. Controlled experimentation reduces product risk by grounding design and feature decisions in empirical data rather than subjective assumptions.

Title: Artificial Intelligence: Large Language Models and Tokenization

Large Language Models (LLMs) have transformed artificial intelligence through advanced natural language processing capabilities. At the core of these transformer-based models is the tokenization process, which breaks raw text strings into smaller sub-word tokens using algorithms like Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) or WordPiece. These tokens are then converted into dense vector embeddings that capture semantic relationships in high-dimensional vector space. The self-attention mechanism allows the model to weigh the contextual importance of different words across an entire sentence simultaneously, regardless of their positional distance. Pre-training on massive text corpora followed by instruction tuning and reinforcement learning from human feedback equips LLMs to generate coherent, context-aware text, translate languages, and solve complex reasoning tasks.

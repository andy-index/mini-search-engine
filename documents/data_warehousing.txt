Modern Data Warehousing and ETL Pipelines

A data warehouse is a centralized repository that stores consolidated, structured data gathered from multiple operational sources across an organization. Unlike transactional databases optimized for fast write operations (OLTP), data warehouses leverage Online Analytical Processing (OLAP) column store architectures to execute complex analytical queries across millions of historical records rapidly. Extract, Transform, Load (ETL) and Extract, Load, Transform (ELT) pipelines automate the flow of data into modern cloud data warehouses like Snowflake, Google BigQuery, and Amazon Redshift. Data modeling paradigms, such as star schemas and snowflake schemas, organize facts and dimension tables to simplify querying and optimize reporting performance.

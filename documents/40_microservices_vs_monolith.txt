Title: Software Engineering: Microservices vs. Monolithic Architecture Trade-offs

Choosing between monolithic and microservices architectures is a critical system design decision with profound organizational and technical implications. A monolithic architecture bundles all business logic, UI, and database interactions into a single deployment unit, offering simplicity in development, testing, and debugging during early project stages. However, as applications grow, monoliths can become tightly coupled and difficult to scale independently. Microservices decompose applications into small, autonomous services communicating over lightweight network protocols. This enables independent scaling, technology diversity, and organizational agility, but introduces distributed system complexities such as network latency, data consistency challenges, and operational overhead in deployment and monitoring.

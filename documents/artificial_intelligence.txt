Artificial Intelligence Overview

Artificial Intelligence (AI) encompasses computer systems designed to emulate human cognitive functions, such as reasoning, learning, problem-solving, perception, and natural language understanding. AI research spans symbolic AI, knowledge representation, expert systems, and statistical learning. Search algorithms like A* search enable pathfinding and decision trees in game-playing engines. Natural Language Processing (NLP) allows algorithms to interpret, generate, and translate human languages using probabilistic models and deep learning architectures. Computer vision enables automated image classification, object detection, and spatial recognition in autonomous systems. Ethical considerations surrounding AI development include algorithmic bias, model explainability, privacy preservation, and safety alignment in critical decision-making applications.

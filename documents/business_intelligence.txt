Business Intelligence and Data Analytics

Business Intelligence (BI) comprises the strategies, architectures, and technologies used by enterprises to analyze raw business data and transform it into actionable strategic insights. Modern BI platforms, such as Tableau, Power BI, and Looker, ingest data from enterprise data warehouses to create interactive dashboards, analytical reports, and key performance indicator (KPI) tracking metrics. Data analysis workflows involve querying structured relational data, identifying historical trends, calculating conversion rates, and performing cohort analyses. Business analysts utilize descriptive statistics and diagnostic analytics to discover underlying operational bottlenecks, measure return on investment across marketing channels, and support data-driven decision-making across executive leadership teams.

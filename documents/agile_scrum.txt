Agile Software Development and Scrum Framework

Agile software development is an iterative approach to project management and software engineering that assists teams in delivering value to customers faster and with fewer headaches. Instead of betting everything on a single big release, an agile team delivers work in small, consumable increments. Scrum is a lightweight framework that helps people, teams, and organizations generate value through adaptive solutions for complex problems. Core Scrum events include Sprint Planning, Daily Stand-ups, Sprint Reviews, and Sprint Retrospectives. Roles in Scrum are strictly defined: Product Owners manage the product backlog and prioritize business value, Scrum Masters facilitate process alignment and remove operational blockers, and cross-functional Development Teams write, test, and deploy software features increment by increment.

Customer Journey Mapping and UX Analysis

Customer journey mapping visualizes the step-by-step process a user undergoes to accomplish a goal when interacting with a digital product or service. UX analysts and product designers map out distinct touchpoints, user goals, pain points, emotional states, and opportunities across discovery, onboarding, engagement, and retention stages. Combining qualitative research methods, such as user interviews and usability testing, with quantitative behavioral data provides a holistic understanding of end-user friction points. Improving user journey friction directly enhances user satisfaction, reduces customer support tickets, and boosts long-term customer loyalty.

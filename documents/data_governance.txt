Data Governance and Quality Assurance

Data governance establishes policies, procedures, and responsibilities to ensure data security, usability, integrity, and compliance across enterprise data assets. Key components include data cataloging, lineage tracking, metadata management, and access control policies. As organizations ingest vast volumes of structured and unstructured data, data quality assurance processes validate accuracy, completeness, consistency, and freshness. Compliance frameworks like GDPR, CCPA, and HIPAA enforce strict data privacy standards, requiring automated data masking, auditing, and consent management. Effective governance ensures analysts and engineers work with reliable, secure data to power business intelligence.

Cryptography and Cryptanalysis

Cryptography is the discipline of securing communication by transforming readable plaintext into unintelligible ciphertext using mathematical algorithms and secret keys. Symmetric encryption, such as Advanced Encryption Standard (AES), utilizes a single shared key for both encryption and decryption, offering high computational speed for bulk data protection. Asymmetric cryptography, or public-key cryptography (e.g., RSA, Elliptic Curve Cryptography), utilizes paired public and private keys for secure key exchange, digital signatures, and identity authentication. Cryptographic hash functions like SHA-256 produce fixed-size outputs that verify data integrity. Cryptanalysis studies methods to analyze or break cryptographic schemes, driving continuous research into post-quantum cryptography resilient to quantum computing attacks.

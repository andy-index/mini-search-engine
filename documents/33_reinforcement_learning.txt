Title: Artificial Intelligence: Reinforcement Learning Fundamentals

Reinforcement learning is a paradigm of artificial intelligence where an autonomous agent learns to make optimal decisions by interacting with an environment to maximize cumulative reward. Key components include the agent, environment, state, action, and reward signal. Through trial and error, guided by the Markov Decision Process (MDP) framework, the agent updates its policy or value functions using algorithms like Q-learning, Policy Gradients, or Deep Q-Networks (DQN). The exploration-exploitation trade-off is central, balancing trying novel actions to discover better strategies versus exploiting known rewarding behaviors. Reinforcement learning drives breakthroughs in autonomous robotics, game playing, and complex resource management systems.

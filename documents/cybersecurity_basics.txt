Principles of Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity involves protecting computer systems, networks, devices, and data from unauthorized access, attacks, or damage. The foundation of information security rests on the CIA triad: Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability. Confidentiality prevents unauthorized data exposure through encryption standards like AES and RSA. Integrity guarantees that information remains untampered using cryptographic hashing functions such as SHA-256. Availability ensures systems remain operational despite hardware failures or distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks. Key security mechanisms include multi-factor authentication (MFA), public key infrastructure (PKI), firewalls, and intrusion detection systems. Regular vulnerability audits, threat modeling, and security awareness training form vital defenses against phishing and malware exploitation.

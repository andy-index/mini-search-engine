Information Retrieval Systems

Information Retrieval (IR) focuses on finding relevant documents or information within large collections of unstructured text based on user search queries. The core data structure powering IR engines is the inverted index, which maps terms and words to lists of documents containing them (postings lists). Document preprocessing includes tokenization, lowercasing, stop-word removal, and stemming or lemmatization. Relevance ranking algorithms calculate term importance using weighting schemes like Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF) or probabilistic models like BM25. Vector space models represent documents and queries as high-dimensional vectors, measuring cosine similarity to compute search result relevance for search engine indexing.

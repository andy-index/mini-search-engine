Predictive Analytics and Data Modeling

Predictive analytics uses historical data, statistical algorithms, and machine learning techniques to forecast future outcomes and probability distributions. In business and product environments, predictive models analyze historical transactional patterns to predict customer churn, estimate future sales demand, detect fraudulent transactions, and deliver personalized product recommendations. Standard analytical workflows involve data preprocessing, feature engineering, model selection (such as random forests or gradient boosting machines), and evaluation using metrics like ROC-AUC, mean squared error, and confusion matrices. Deploying predictive models into production allows real-time automated decision-making.

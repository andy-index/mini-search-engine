Product Lifecycle Management

Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) encompasses the strategic administration of a product as it moves through four distinct stages: Introduction, Growth, Maturity, and Decline. During the introduction phase, product managers focus on establishing market validation and reaching Product-Market Fit (PMF). The growth stage prioritizes scaling customer acquisition, expanding feature sets, and defending market position against emerging competitors. Maturity shifts focus toward optimizing user retention, monetizing active user bases, and refining operational efficiency. Finally, during decline, product teams decide whether to pivot, harvest, or sunset sunsetting software systems. Strategic roadmap planning aligns engineering execution with product stage goals.

Introduction to Quantum Computing

Quantum computing is an emerging technology that applies principles of quantum mechanics to solve complex computational problems faster than classical supercomputers. Instead of classical binary bits that store values of 0 or 1, quantum computers utilize quantum bits (qubits). Superposition allows qubits to represent combinations of 0 and 1 simultaneously, while quantum entanglement links qubit states instantaneously across physical distances. Quantum algorithms like Shor's algorithm for prime factorization and Grover's algorithm for unstructured database searching demonstrate exponential speedups over classical algorithms. Hardware implementations involve superconducting circuits, trapped ions, and photonic platforms, requiring extreme cooling and advanced quantum error correction protocols.
